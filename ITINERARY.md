# LONDON

## Saturday, Oct 10 — Sarah lands, London on foot

Sarah's overnight flight lands Heathrow early morning. Piccadilly line into town — cheap, and only marginally slower than the express.

Today is deliberately low-effort because she'll be wrecked. All walking, all free:
- **Westminster & Big Ben**, then across Westminster Bridge for the classic view
- **Thames South Bank walk** east past the London Eye
- **Tower Bridge** and the Tower of London from outside
- **Trafalgar Square** and **Buckingham Palace** if energy holds

Stay outside in daylight as long as possible — it's the only thing that actually fixes jet lag. Sunrise 7:20am, sunset 6:15pm.

**Dinner:** Dishoom. Book ahead. Black daal and the chai.

## Sunday, Oct 11 — Church + temple grounds

- **Morning: Sacrament meeting at the Hyde Park Chapel**, 64–68 Exhibition Road, South Kensington. South Kensington tube. The building also houses a visitors' center with exhibits on temples and the history of the Church in the British Isles — worth 30 minutes after the block.

- **Afternoon: London England Temple grounds**, West Park Road, Newchapel, Lingfield, Surrey RH7 6HW.
  - Train from London Victoria or London Bridge toward East Grinstead or Lingfield, roughly 50 minutes.
  - Lingfield station is 2 miles from the temple with **no bus service** — taxi or rideshare the last stretch. Arrange the return ride before you're dropped off; it's rural.
  - Grounds are open to all during daylight hours. Formal gardens, a large pond, and an oak on site that's over 450 years old — President McKay had it preserved when he chose the building site.
  - The temple itself is closed Sundays, so this is a grounds visit only. Plan to be there well before sunset at 6:10pm.

- **Evening back in London:** Padella in Borough for pasta — best cheap meal in the city. No reservations; go at 5pm.

**Alternate if you want an actual session:** see the Tuesday variant at the end of this document.

## Monday, Oct 12 — Stonehenge

The budget route beats the tour coaches:
- Train **London Waterloo → Salisbury** (~90 min). Book advance tickets; walk-up fare is several times higher.
- **The Stonehenge Tour** bus runs from Salisbury station and the fare bundles site admission.
- Back in London by early evening.

**Optional upgrade:** English Heritage's Stone Circle Experience puts you inside the ring outside normal hours. Check October availability directly with English Heritage — some operators indicate special access isn't offered in October or November, so treat this as a bonus rather than a plan. About $150 for the two of you.

**Evening:** Pack. Early flight tomorrow.

# ICELAND

**Every night here is an aurora night.** The aurora section below is the most important page in this document.

## Tuesday, Oct 13 — Fly + volcano country

- Morning flight LHR→KEF, ~3 hours. Iceland is an hour behind London; you land around midday.
- **Pick up the rental car at KEF.** A 2WD handles this entire route in mid-October and saves real money. Take the gravel and sand/ash damage waiver — that's a genuine Iceland risk, not an upsell.

**Afternoon on the Reykjanes Peninsula** — you're already on it; it's the airport peninsula.
- **Sundhnúkur / Fagradalsfjall lava fields.** Marked trails cross lava from the 2021–2025 eruptions; some of it still radiates heat. **Check safetravel.is and almannavarnir.is before driving out** — exclusion zones move.
- **Bridge Between Continents** — footbridge over a rift between the North American and Eurasian plates. Ten minutes, on the way.
- **Gunnuhver** — violently boiling mud pots and steam vents.

- Check into Reykjavík. **Dinner:** Messinn for fish, or Bæjarins Beztu for the hot dog.
- **Aurora night 1:** Grótta lighthouse, Seltjarnarnes. Fifteen minutes from downtown, free parking, dark enough to work.

Sunrise 8:12am, sunset 6:05pm.

## Wednesday, Oct 14 — Golden Circle + inside the glacier

Big day. Roll at 8:30.

- **Þingvellir National Park** (9:00) — walk the Almannagjá rift where the plates pull apart. This is the tectonic plates park. Parking fee applies.
- **Geysir** (10:45) — Strokkur erupts every 5–10 minutes, dependably.
- **Gullfoss** (11:45) — the two-tier falls. Quick stop; you're coming back here.
- **Into the Glacier, departing Gullfoss** (13:00, ~3.5 hrs) — Sleipnir monster truck up onto Langjökull, then an hour walking through 1,600 feet of blue ice tunnel carved into the glacier, including the ice chapel. Thermal suits and helmets provided. Book direct; the Gullfoss departure is far cheaper than the Reykjavík pickup version.
- **Secret Lagoon, Flúðir** (~17:15) — Iceland's oldest swimming pool, built 1891, in a geothermal field with a small geyser erupting alongside. Your tourist hot spring, at a fraction of Blue Lagoon's price and with more character.
- Drive to the guesthouse near **Hella or Hvolsvöllur**. Two nights.
- **Aurora night 2** — rural and dark. Statistically your best shot.

Sunrise 8:15am, sunset 6:01pm.

## Thursday, Oct 15 — South Coast + molten lava

- **Seljalandsfoss** — the waterfall you walk behind. You will get drenched. Rain shells mandatory.
- **Skógafoss** — 200 feet, walk right to the base.
- **Dyrhólaey** — headland arch with the whole black coastline below.
- **Reynisfjara black sand beach**, Vík — basalt columns and sea stacks.

> **Safety, not boilerplate:** Reynisfjara kills tourists every few years. Sneaker waves arrive with no warning and drag people out. Stay well up the beach, never turn your back on the water, and do not walk to the waterline for a photo.

- **Lava Show, Vík** (evening, ~1 hr) — they superheat real basalt to 1,100°C and pour glowing lava into the room in front of you. You feel the heat on your face. With no eruption running, this is the closest you'll get, and it stands on its own merits.
- Back to the guesthouse. **Aurora night 3.**

Sunrise 8:18am, sunset 5:58pm.

## Friday, Oct 16 — Hot river + horses

- **Reykjadalur, Hveragerði** (morning). Park at the trailhead and hike about 2 miles uphill through a steaming geothermal valley, 45–60 minutes. At the top, a river you sit down in, with boardwalks and changing screens. The water gets hotter further upstream — find your spot. **Your non-tourist hot spring.** Free, and the most Icelandic thing on this itinerary.
- **Icelandic horse riding** (afternoon, 1–2 hrs) at a farm near Hveragerði or Selfoss. Ask specifically for a ride that demonstrates the **tölt** — the fifth gait unique to the breed, smooth enough to hold a drink while riding. Beginners are fine; these horses are famously calm.
- Drive to lodging near Keflavík (~1 hr) for the early flight.
- **Aurora night 4** — the Reykjanes lava fields are dark and ten minutes from the airport hotels.

Sunrise 8:21am, sunset 5:54pm.

## Saturday, Oct 17 — Home

Early Icelandair KEF→Chicago O'Hare, connect to Madison. Home early afternoon.

# THE AURORA PLAN

This determines whether the trip delivers. Read it twice.

**Your conditions are good.** New moon is October 10, so all four Iceland nights have a thin crescent that sets early — essentially zero moonlight. Full darkness from about 7:30pm to 7:30am. Iceland sits directly under the auroral oval, so you don't need a major storm; Kp 2–3 is enough.

**Every morning, check `vedur.is/en/weather/aurora`.** It shows aurora activity *and* a cloud cover map. **The cloud map matters more.** Clouds, not solar activity, are what ruin aurora trips.

**If your area is socked in, drive.** Iceland's weather changes completely over 50 miles. A clear window is often an hour away, and you have a car specifically so you can chase it.

**Be outside and dark-adapted 9pm–1am.** Twenty full minutes with no phone screen before you decide nothing is happening.

**Expectation setting:** a moderate aurora looks like a pale grey-green smudge to the naked eye and only goes vivid green on camera. A strong one is unmistakable and ripples like fabric. Both are worth the cold. Don't write off night one because it didn't look like the postcard.

**Phone camera:** night mode, 10-second exposure, propped on a rock or a cheap tripod. That tripod is the best packing decision you'll make.

**Stargazing bonus:** the same dark skies on nights 2 and 3 give you the Milky Way with no effort.

# VOLCANO CONTINGENCY

No active eruption as of now. The last ran July 16 to early August 2025 — the ninth in the Sundhnúksgígar crater row. Magma continues accumulating beneath Svartsengi at record levels and the Icelandic Met Office considers another intrusion, possibly another eruption, the most likely outcome. Nobody can say whether that lands in October 2026.

**If one starts while you're there:**
1. **Check almannavarnir.is immediately.** Civil Protection controls access, and early-stage eruptions are typically closed to the public for days.
2. **If ground access opens**, hikes from the Reykjanes trailheads run 2–4 hours round trip over rough lava. Free.
3. **Helicopter tours** launch within a day or two and give the best views, at $400–600 per person. That breaks the budget — your call in the moment.
4. **Never approach a fresh flow from downwind.** Volcanic gas concentrations near vents are lethal, and that's how people actually die at these sites.

# APPENDIX: Tuesday Temple Session Variant

If you'd rather attend an actual session than just walk the grounds:

- Move Sunday afternoon to something in London (Greenwich, British Museum).
- **Tuesday Oct 13 morning:** temple session at Newchapel. Reservations available, walk-ins welcome, but without a reservation you may be asked to wait or participate in a different ordinance. Bring your own temple clothing; rental is available.
- **Fly to Iceland from Gatwick instead of Heathrow** — the temple is a few miles west of it. Play and easyJet both serve LGW→KEF.
- **Knock-on effect:** you land in Iceland in the evening rather than midday, so Tuesday becomes drive-and-aurora only. Move the Reykjanes lava fields, Bridge Between Continents, and Gunnuhver to Friday afternoon, before checking in near Keflavík.
- **Tradeoff:** Friday gets tight — Reykjadalur, horses, and Reykjanes in one day, with the lava fields in fading light. Worth it if the session matters more than the volcano stop.
